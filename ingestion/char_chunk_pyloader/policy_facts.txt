LIC’s NEW CHILDREN’s MONEY BACK PLAN (UIN: 512N296V03)
============================================================

[PLAN TYPE]
- A Par, Non Linked, Life, Individual, Savings Plan

[DEATH BENEFIT]
- Sum Assured on Death:
  - higher of:
    - Basic Sum Assured
    - 7 times annualized premium

- Minimum guarantee:
  - not less than 105% of total premiums paid upto date of death

[MINOR RISK CONDITION]
- For child below age 8 years:
  - risk commences:
    - 2 years from commencement of policy
    - or when child attains age 8 years
  - whichever is earlier

[SURVIVAL BENEFIT]
- 20% of Basic Sum Assured

- Payable at:
  - age 18 years
  - age 20 years
  - age 22 years

[SURVIVAL BENEFIT DEFERMENT]
- Survival Benefits may be deferred
- Deferred Survival Benefits payable later with additional amount

[POLICY LOAN]
- Loan available after:
  - completion of first policy year
  - provided atleast one full year’s premium paid

- Maximum loan:
  - In-force:
    - 75%
    - 90%

  - Paid-up:
    - 60%
    - 80%

[PREMIUM GRACE PERIOD]
- 30 days:
  - yearly
  - half-yearly
  - quarterly

- 15 days:
  - monthly

[FREE LOOK PERIOD]
- 30 days
